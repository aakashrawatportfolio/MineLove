For Jaya

To The Most Special Person In My Life ❤️

Some feelings are hard to explain, but they stay forever.

Dear Jaya,

I don’t always know how to say things perfectly, but I know one thing clearly — you are very special to me. You are the only girl I have loved this deeply in my entire life.

I have a few dreams, and I don’t know what life has planned ahead, but deep down, I wish those dreams had you in them. I know you don’t always like daily calls, daily conversations, or too much emotional pressure, and I respect that.

I also know that things are different in our lives, but my feelings for you have always been very strong and real. I have always wanted you to share your thoughts, your fears, your happiness, and your little moments with me.

One thing that truly hurt me was when you said there was never anything between us. That moment made me question myself — was I really so bad that I didn’t even deserve that place in your heart?

You are the person who showed me the real meaning of love. I know I am not perfect. I may say silly things, I may act immature sometimes, but my emotions for you have always been honest.

You have seen the real Aakash — the version that not everyone gets to see. And without even realising it, you have done so much for me. Thank you for every memory, every conversation, every meeting, and every moment.

I have kept all our memories safely with me — whether it is our chats, our meetings, or the little things connected to you. They mean a lot to me.

I just pray to God that things become better and, for as long as we are connected, we create good memories. Maybe one day I may suddenly disappear from your life without saying much, but I want you to remember that there was someone named Aakash who truly cared for you.

I really want you to share things with me. I know you are private, and I respect that, but somewhere in my heart, I still wish to be someone you can trust.

I am sorry for anything I ever said or did that hurt you. That was never my intention. You will always remain special to me.
